# HiveOS AI Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 25, 2026

HiveOS AI is designed as an offline-friendly beekeeping intelligence and record-keeping app. The app stores apiary records, hive notes, inspections, photos, audio placeholders, reports, subscription status, and app preferences locally on your device unless a future cloud feature is explicitly enabled.

## Data Collection

HiveOS AI does not sell user data and does not use third-party advertising trackers.

The current production app uses mock AI by default. Hive, inspection, image, audio, and weather-placeholder data are processed locally for app features and are not sent to HiveOS AI servers in the default configuration.

## User-Provided Content

You may choose to add hive names, apiary locations, notes, photos, inspection records, swarm sighting notes, and audio placeholders. This information is used to provide app features such as dashboards, reminders, reports, charts, and informational hive insights.

## Permissions

HiveOS AI may request access to:

- Camera and Photo Library, to attach hive and inspection images.
- Microphone and Speech Recognition, to support voice notes and audio-intelligence placeholders.
- Notifications, to provide inspection reminders and swarm-alert placeholders.

You can manage these permissions in iOS Settings.

## Subscriptions

Subscriptions are handled by Apple through StoreKit. HiveOS AI does not directly process payment card details.

## Agricultural Disclaimer

HiveOS AI provides informational agricultural insights only. It does not provide veterinary advice, guaranteed disease detection, guaranteed colony outcomes, or regulatory certification. Users should independently verify hive decisions and consult qualified local experts where appropriate.
