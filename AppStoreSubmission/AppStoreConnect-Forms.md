Description:

```text
HiveOS AI is the operating system for intelligent beekeeping.

Designed for hobbyists, professional beekeepers, apiary operators, honey farms and agricultural businesses, HiveOS AI brings hive records, inspections, swarm awareness, weather planning, analytics and premium AI-assisted insights into one elegant mobile workspace.

Track apiaries and hive profiles, record inspections, organize queen notes, review productivity trends, attach hive photos, generate local PDF reports and monitor colony status with Hive Pulse.

Core features:

- Hive health dashboard
- Apiary and hive management
- Inspection checklists and history
- AI Hive Scan placeholder with cautious informational insights
- Hive Audio Intelligence placeholder
- Hive Pulse status system
- Swarm Alert Network for local swarm sighting records and notification scaffolding
- Weather intelligence placeholder
- Honey production tracking placeholders
- Swift Charts analytics
- Native PDF reports and sharing
- Local notification reminders
- Multilingual app guidance
- Voice input for editable inspection, hive, apiary and swarm sighting notes
- Offline-friendly local data storage

HiveOS AI is built for modern beekeeping operations that need clearer records, better seasonal visibility and faster awareness of possible hive issues.

Important agricultural disclaimer:
HiveOS AI provides informational agricultural insights only. It does not provide veterinary advice, guaranteed disease detection, guaranteed colony outcomes or regulatory certification. Users should independently verify all hive decisions and consult qualified local experts where appropriate.
```
